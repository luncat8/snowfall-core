VN mode — five ways to read the same story

The question: when the reader does not scroll and everything sits on one screen, what moves the pictures, the theme and the scripts? Each page below answers it differently on the unchanged engine and the same story. Every page has a book ⇄ VN switch, a script log that flags anything out of order, a speed control, and a self-test button that walks the story both ways.

Two ways to take the scroll away

Orthogonal to the variant, switchable in each toolbar (A–D):

  • window, locked — the window is still the scrollport, the reader is locked out (overflow: hidden), the controller scrolls it. The engine runs unchanged.
  • stage, no window scroll — the window never scrolls at all; a fixed stage is the scrollport. Needed if the story must live inside a page that has other content. Costs the engine a “which element scrolls” option.

What to look at

  • Red → green: green's top edge meets red's bottom edge and pushes it out — never an overlap, in both directions.
  • Green → blue → gold: the same push with a left, a right and a bottom exit.
  • Blue → gold: the page turns from night to day gradually; the toolbar and log take the theme too.
  • The log: scripts count 1…12; a red line means one fired out of order or was skipped.
  • Gold: the story asks a question and waits for the answer.
  • Blue's long portion and violet's small picture are the awkward cases.
  • Back (Backspace) at any point, and book ⇄ VN (key v) at any point: the place is kept.
  • Speed “slow ×4” makes every push easy to inspect.
