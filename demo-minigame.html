the coin road

You start with 100 gold. Twice on this road a coin is offered: call ×0.5 and the purse halves, call ×2 and it doubles. Walk past without calling and the game's own default pays — ×2.

The coin is one file, games/gamble.js plus games/gamble.css, registered with SnowfallGames.add and played with SnowfallGames.play. This page never writes story state: the engine records every resolution and the purse is 100 × Snowfall.product('mult.').

Your purse is waiting.

Multipliers are recorded like any other outcome: live when you call the coin, saved when a later visit replays your own earlier call, default when you walked past and the game's declared fallback paid. The purse is a pure function of the store — 100 × product('mult.') — so a re-pass that answers nothing changes nothing, and answering again overwrites that seat's factor instead of stacking it. Jump over a seat with the numbers above to keep it. Clearing the save at the top re-arms whatever stands on screen, so seat 1 offers the coin again right away. This page only reads product and draws it.
